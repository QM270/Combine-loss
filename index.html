How to check your loss

  1. Find your concentration factor. CF = cut width ÷ discharge width. Choppers and spreaders off.
  2. Collect a sample of known area from the discharge behind the combine. Stop and shut down before placing or retrieving the pan — never reach into a running machine.
  3. Clean the seed out of the sample: sieve it through a screen or blow the chaff out. A leaf blower and an 85 L tub works well.
  4. Weigh, measure or count the clean seed.
  5. Divide by your pan area to get the amount per square foot.
  6. Read the loss per acre off the tables below — or just use the Loss tab, which does all of this for you.

Sample in front of the combine too. That catches header and pre-harvest shatter loss, which no combine adjustment will fix. Subtract it from your total.

Where is the loss coming from?

Make one adjustment at a time, then re-check.

Start up

Set to the manual / monitor pre-set for the crop. Feed the combine slowly and increase fan speed until you just start blowing a few seeds over the chaffer.

Under-threshing

Look for: seed still in heads in the straw, unthreshed heads at the cleaner or in the returns, part heads in the grain tank.
Fix: increase threshing speed, decrease concave clearance, add concave blanks, slow down.

Over-threshing

Look for: no unthreshed heads but broken chaff-like straw, high chaff load at the cleaner, cracked grain in the tank or returns.
Fix: drive faster, decrease threshing speed, increase concave clearance.

Separating

Free grain in the straw: increase threshing speed, decrease concave clearance, use wider-spaced wire concaves, reduce vane angle, slow down.
Excessive chaff in the straw: decrease threshing speed, increase concave clearance, use narrow wire-spaced concaves, increase vane angle.

Cleaning

Threshed seed going out with the chaff: increase chaffer sieve opening, even out chaff/grain loading, decrease threshing, slow down.
Light trash in the grain: increase fan speed, decrease chaffer opening, decrease cleaning sieve opening.
Clean grain in the returns: open the sieve, open the chaffer, decrease fan speed.

Reference

Methods and tables adapted from the Combine Seed Loss Guide, Prairie Agricultural Machinery Institute (PAMI) and the Canola Council of Canada. Calculator logic follows the farmpro.ca combine loss calculators.

Volume figures use the Imperial bushel (36.3687 L). This app runs entirely on your phone — no internet needed, no data sent anywhere.
